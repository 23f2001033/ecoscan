# EcoScan — Demo Script (~2 min)

Short lines. Say them in your own words — don't read. Show more than you talk.

**Setup:** open https://ecoscan-oregon.vercel.app · have the **ivy photo** and the **car photo** ready ·
do one practice scan first · leave ~30s between scans on camera.

---

### 0:00 – 0:20 · Problem

> "Invasive plants are destroying Oregon habitat. English ivy pulls down native trees.
>
> People volunteer to remove them. The problem is telling them apart — our state flower looks like an
> invasive weed.
>
> Pull the wrong one and you destroy what you came to protect."

---

### 0:20 – 1:10 · Demo — *let the screen do the work*

**Upload the ivy photo.**

> "One photo. Two seconds."

**Result appears. Point at things.**

> "English ivy. 95% confident — and it says why: glossy lobed leaves, pale veins.
>
> Invasive. Impact 8 out of 10. Here's how to remove it.
>
> And that advice is from the Oregon Department of Agriculture — not from the AI."

**Now upload the car photo.**

> "Now something that isn't a plant."

**Refusal appears. Pause. Let it land.**

> "It says no. It doesn't guess."

---

### 1:10 – 1:50 · The one idea

> "That's the whole design.
>
> The AI answers one question — what plant is this. It never writes the removal advice. That comes from
> my own database: 14 Oregon species, every entry sourced.
>
> Because a made-up plant name is a bug. Made-up herbicide advice is a hazard.
>
> So it gives three honest answers: here's the plant and what to do, or here's the plant but I don't
> have verified guidance, or I'm not sure — instead of one confident guess."

**If asked "why not just use iNaturalist / Seek?" — answer it here, unprompted:**

> "Seek tells you what a plant is. EcoScan tells you what to do about it — in Oregon, with a source.
> Identification is the input, not the product."

---

### 1:50 – 2:05 · Close

> "It works on a phone, with a keyboard, and with a screen reader.
>
> Oregon doesn't need more people willing to help. It needs them to know what they're looking at.
>
> That's EcoScan."

---

## Guardrails

**True — safe to say:** "under two seconds" · "14 species with sourced guidance" · "the model identifies,
the database advises" · "it refuses when it isn't sure"

**Don't say:** any accuracy percentage · "works on any plant" · any cost statistic you haven't looked up

**If a scan misfires on camera — keep it in** and say *"and that's why we show confidence."* A handled
failure looks more real than a perfect demo.
